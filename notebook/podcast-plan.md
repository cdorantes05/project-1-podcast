The plan for the episode: three 10-minute parts, each with its own look, and the questions for each part. Converted from `Podcast.pdf`.

## Aesthetics

- Using laptop Photo Booth

- Lighting: Christmas lights, lamp lights

## Topics

### Part I – Real life, becoming an adult; advice

- When was the first time you felt like a real adult? When was the last time you definitely didn't?
- How is your relationship with your family different now from when you left for college?
- What's a habit you're trying to build before you graduate?
- What's the most "I have no idea what I'm doing" moment you've had this year?
- What's a mistake you're actually glad you made?

### Part II – College, looking back

Hot takes: overrated and underrated things on campus.

- Cafe Gato Rojo
- Comping The Crimson
- Studying abroad
- The Quad
- Working for The Harvard Shop
- River Run
- The Widener Stacks
- Grendsdays
- Electric scooters
- Harvard-Yale Tailgate
- Harvard Shuttles

Then:

- What would you tell yourself on move-in day?
- What's something you'll miss that sounds boring (a dining hall, a walk, a study spot)?
- What's the biggest way this place changed you?
- What are you most scared of about leaving? What are you most excited about?

### Part III – Identity and growing up

- Your most-played song of each year.
- What's something you believed at 18 that you've changed your mind about?
- What part of yourself did you find in college that you didn't know was there?
- What's something you're still figuring out and are OK not having figured out?
- When did you last surprise yourself?
