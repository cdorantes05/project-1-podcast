## Common threads

**Style.** The samples point to a cosy, personal, slightly nostalgic look: bedrooms, couches and kitchens instead of studios, with Y2K and desktop-interface graphics and handmade collage. The visual references are Gen-Z internet culture (Emma Chamberlain, Olivia Rodrigo, old Windows interfaces).

**Topics.** Pop culture, fashion and celebrity, friendship and sisterhood, identity and growing up ("lower your expectations about people", "Who am I?"), and music.

**Format.** A video-first conversation podcast. Most of the references are an interview or two co-hosts talking. Full episodes run 30 to 70 minutes. Each show has one clear hook and a signature setting. The lyric-video material adds a second layer: short, captioned, highly designed clips.

### Takeaways for Carolyn's show

1. **Make it a video podcast, filmed somewhere real.** A dorm room, a bed or a couch, with posters and personal items in frame, will look more like these references than a rented studio. Keep the mics and headphones visible.
2. **Pick a one-line hook the way each reference show does.** Examples are therapy-couch fashion talk, hot takes, or cocktails with Ina. A campus version might be "confessions from Harvard Yard" or "defend your take" with classmates.
3. **Go conversational, ideally with a co-host or a rotating guest.** Five of the six playlist episodes and most of the board images show two people. A friend co-host gives the "Friends Keep Secrets" chemistry; solo episodes can be occasional, like Emma Chamberlain's.
4. **Aim for 30 to 50 minutes per full episode.** Then cut 30 to 60 second clips for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, about the length of the lyric-video references.
5. **Use the lyric-video look for clips and captions.** Use animated, hand-drawn or Y2K-style captions, quote cards that look like Windows pop-ups, and folder or desktop layouts for episode menus or guest intros.
6. **Write titles as stories, not topics.** Use lowercase, conversational titles built around one striking detail ("X and Y met in an Air France bathroom").
7. **Build one visual system across cover art, thumbnails and clips.** Mix scrapbook and collage with desktop-interface graphics, using warm muted photos and pink, green and yellow accents. Reusing the same frames (CRT, phone screen, Paint window) across clips makes them recognisable.
8. **Cover culture, identity and friendship.** Fashion, music, campus life and "who am I becoming" questions fit everything she collected.
